=== CHANGELOG (Nexus "Changelogs" tab) ===
0.3.0
- New: search box on the Inventory tab's items gallery (task items, keys, recipes, blueprints). Type to filter every section by name; recipes also match the item they make. Game hotkeys are muted while typing; Enter keeps the filter, Esc or the x clears it.
- Marked recipes and blueprints are pinned to the top of their gallery section.
- Marking from anywhere refreshes every open panel right away.
- More reliable hooking of the worktable blueprint grid and the gallery.
